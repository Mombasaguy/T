## 💡 What This Means for Your App

### Good News ✅

2. You don't need to filter domains or parse profiles

### Reality Check ⚠️
1. **Results will be heavily LinkedIn** (that's what works best)
2. **You'll also get**: GitHub profiles, personal blogs, company bios
3. **You might NOT find**: People without public profiles
