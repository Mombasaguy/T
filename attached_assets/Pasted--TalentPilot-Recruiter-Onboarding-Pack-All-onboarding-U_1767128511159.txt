# TalentPilot – Recruiter Onboarding Pack
_All onboarding UX flow, screens, microcopy, nudges, and demo alignment_

---

# =========================
# ONBOARDING GOAL
# =========================

Primary success metric:
> User runs a search → clicks a candidate → understands why the candidate surfaced.

Secondary success metric:
> User saves at least one candidate.

Everything below is designed to achieve those two outcomes as fast as possible.

---

# =========================
# STEP 1: WELCOME SCREEN
# =========================

## Title
Welcome to TalentPilot

## Body Copy
TalentPilot helps recruiters find qualified candidates beyond job boards by searching across public professional work.

Start by describing the person you’re looking for. We’ll take care of the rest.

## Primary Button
Run Your First Search

## Notes
- No videos
- No setup questions
- No personalization forms
- No AI explanations

Purpose: calm confidence and immediate action.

---

# =========================
# STEP 2: FIRST SEARCH (GUIDED)
# =========================

## Search Input (Pre-filled, Editable)
Senior backend engineer with fintech experience who’s worked on payments systems

## Helper Text (Below Input)
Write this the way you’d explain the role to a colleague. You can edit this at any time.

## Search Button
Search Candidates

## Notes
- Always pre-fill with a realistic example
- Make it editable
- Avoid blank states

---

# =========================
# STEP 3: SEARCH LOADING STATE
# =========================

## Loading Message
Searching public professional profiles…

## Subtext
Looking at experience, work, and relevant professional signals.

Purpose: trust-building while results load.

---

# =========================
# STEP 4: RESULTS EDUCATION (INLINE ONLY)
# =========================

## Match Badge Tooltip
Indicates how closely this candidate matches your search based on experience and demonstrated work.

## Relevance Score Tooltip
Reflects how well this profile aligns with your search description.

## Candidate Panel Hint
This panel explains why the candidate surfaced and highlights relevant experience.

Notes:
- No modal tours
- No step-by-step walkthrough
- Just small, contextual explanations

---

# =========================
# STEP 5: FIRST “AHA” MOMENT
# =========================

## Highlight Callout (On First Candidate Click)
This candidate surfaced because of relevant experience in fintech systems and hands-on work with payment infrastructure.

Purpose:
Connect the user’s words → candidate → trust.

---

# =========================
# STEP 6: FIRST ACTION PROMPT
# =========================

After viewing a candidate for ~10–15 seconds:

## Prompt
Save this candidate to review later?

## Primary Action
Save Candidate

## Secondary (Optional)
Add a quick note

Notes:
- Do NOT push outreach
- Do NOT push upgrades
- Do NOT push team invites

---

# =========================
# FREE PLAN USAGE NUDGES
# =========================

## After 4 Searches (Soft, Informational)
You’ve used 4 of your 10 free searches. Most recruiters refine their searches a few times before finding strong matches.

## After 8 Searches (Social Proof)
Recruiting teams usually upgrade once they’ve identified candidates they want to follow up with.

## After 10 Searches (Hard Wall)
You’ve used your free searches.

Upgrade to continue sourcing, saving, and managing candidates.

[View Plans]

Tone:
- Calm
- Fair
- Non-urgent
- No guilt

---

# =========================
# EMPTY & EDGE STATES
# =========================

## Empty Search State
Describe the candidate you’re looking for — experience, background, or outcomes.

## No Results State
Try broadening your description or focusing on experience rather than titles.

## No Saved Candidates
Saved candidates will appear here so you can review them later.

# ONBOARDING → DEMO ALIGNMENT

## 30-Second Demo Script (Matches Onboarding)

“TalentPilot helps recruiters find qualified candidates beyond job boards by searching across public professional work.”

Search:
“I describe the role in plain English — the way I already think about candidates.”

Results:
“Instead of a long list, I get a focused shortlist ranked by relevance.”

Close:

---

# =========================
# METRICS TO TRACK
# =========================

Activation Metrics:
- % of users who run first search
- % who click a candidate
- % who save at least one candidate

Conversion Signals:
- Searches per user
- Time to first save
- Searches before upgrade

Drop-Off Warnings:
- Users who search but never click a candidate
- Users who click but don’t understand relevance

---

# =========================
# WHAT NOT TO BUILD (YET)
# =========================

- Multi-step onboarding wizards
- Mandatory profile setup
- AI explanation screens
- Feature tours
- Outreach-first onboarding

---

# =========================
# ONBOARDING PHILOSOPHY
# =========================

TalentPilot onboarding should feel like:
“This tool understands how recruiters think.”

Not:
“This tool is teaching me how to use AI.”
